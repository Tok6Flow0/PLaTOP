# PLaTOP

**P**ocket **L**ab of **a**gents · **T**op-down **O**ffice **P**layground

- **Pocket Lab** — a small window that stays on your desktop, floating or minimised. It's somewhere to watch the thing being investigated here: automated chats that work as a team, and the team identities they give themselves when you ask them to.
- **agents** — the lowercase *a* is on purpose. The capitals name the place; the agents are the little ones who live in it.
- **Top-down Office Playground** — the Sims-style view of the office they share. They wander, work, chat when they pass each other, and fall asleep with a few Zs when their chat goes quiet.

## Saying it

*pla-TOP* — a light first syllable, then a little pop on the second.

That's also the shape of the sound the name came from. The founder's original recording has the same two humps in every syllable: a soft *pla* and a popped *top*. The characters' voices are synthesised from measurements of that recording (see [SOUND.md](SOUND.md)), so when they chatter they're saying the app's name to each other.

## Writing it

Always `PLaTOP`: capital P and L, lowercase a, capital T, O, P. In code identifiers use `platop`.
